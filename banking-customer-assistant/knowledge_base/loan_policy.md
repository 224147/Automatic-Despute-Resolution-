# Loan Policy

EMI payments are due on the date shown in the loan summary each month. A missed EMI payment may attract a late fee of ₹500 and can affect the customer's credit score.

Foreclosure (early repayment) of a personal loan is allowed after 12 months from disbursement, subject to a foreclosure charge of 2% of the outstanding principal. Home loans can be foreclosed at any time without a foreclosure charge, in line with regulatory guidelines for floating-rate loans.
