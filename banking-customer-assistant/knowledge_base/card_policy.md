# Card Policy

Debit and credit cards issued on demo accounts can be blocked instantly by the customer through this assistant. A blocked card cannot be unblocked automatically for security reasons if it was blocked due to a reported loss or theft — those cases require a replacement card, which takes 5-7 business days to arrive.

Cards that have expired cannot be blocked or unblocked; they must be replaced. There is no fee for blocking a card. Unblocking a card that was blocked by the customer voluntarily is free and instant.
