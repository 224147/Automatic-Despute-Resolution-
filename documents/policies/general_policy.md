# General Banking Dispute Policy

Policy ID: POL-GEN-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2024-01-01
Category: GENERAL

## 1. NEFT/RTGS/IMPS Transaction Disputes

### 1.1 Failed Transfer – Amount Debited
- NEFT/RTGS/IMPS transactions that fail at beneficiary bank must be auto-reversed within T+1 day (IMPS/RTGS) or T+2 days (NEFT).
- If not reversed within the timeline, customer may raise dispute.

### 1.2 Auto-Resolution
- Amount ≤ INR 50,000: eligible for auto-reversal.
- Amount > INR 50,000: requires operations team review.
- Beneficiary bank confirmation required for all amounts.

## 2. Wrong Bank Charges/Fees

### 2.1 Eligibility
- Customer charged incorrectly for services.
- Duplicate fee deduction.
- Charges for services not availed.

### 2.2 Resolution
- Verified incorrect charges ≤ INR 5,000: auto-reverse.
- All other cases: manual review by operations.
- Customer must specify which charge is disputed.

## 3. Loan EMI Disputes

### 3.1 Common Issues
- Double EMI deduction.
- EMI debited after loan closure.
- Incorrect EMI amount.

### 3.2 Resolution
- Double deduction confirmed by system: auto-reverse excess amount.
- EMI after closure: verify loan closure date, auto-reverse if confirmed.
- Incorrect amount: manual review by loan operations team.
- All loan disputes > INR 25,000: escalate to loan operations.

## 4. General Rules
- All disputes must be acknowledged within 24 hours.
- Resolution TAT: 5-7 business days for standard disputes.
- Complex disputes: up to 30 days with interim updates every 7 days.
- Customer must be notified at every stage of dispute resolution.
- All actions must be audit-logged.
