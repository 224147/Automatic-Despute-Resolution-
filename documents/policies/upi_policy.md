# UPI Transaction Dispute Policy

Policy ID: POL-UPI-001
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: 2024-01-01
Category: UPI

## 1. UPI Transaction Failed – Amount Debited

### 1.1 Eligibility for Auto-Reversal
- If a UPI transaction fails at the beneficiary bank but the remitter's account is debited, the amount shall be auto-reversed within T+5 business days as per RBI guidelines.
- If the amount is not reversed within T+5 days, the customer may raise a dispute.

### 1.2 Resolution Timeline
- Complaints must be resolved within 5 business days of filing.
- If resolution is not possible within 5 days, provisional credit must be provided within T+5 days.

### 1.3 Auto-Resolution Criteria
- Transaction status confirmed as FAILED at beneficiary end.
- Amount ≤ INR 25,000: eligible for auto-reversal without manual review.
- Amount > INR 25,000 and ≤ INR 100,000: eligible for auto-reversal with system verification.
- Amount > INR 100,000: requires manual review by dispute team.

### 1.4 Required Verification
- Customer identity verification.
- Transaction reference validation.
- Beneficiary bank response confirmation.

## 2. UPI Transaction Pending

### 2.1 Pending Transaction Handling
- Transactions pending for more than 48 hours should be flagged for review.
- If transaction is still pending after 72 hours, auto-reversal should be initiated.

### 2.2 Resolution
- Pending transactions under INR 10,000: auto-reverse after 48 hours.
- Pending transactions over INR 10,000: escalate to operations team.

## 3. Fraud Prevention
- Any UPI dispute with suspected fraud indicators must be escalated immediately.
- Multiple UPI disputes from the same customer within 30 days: flag for review.
- UPI transactions to flagged merchants: enhanced scrutiny required.
